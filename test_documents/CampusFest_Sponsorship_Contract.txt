CAMPUSFEST 2026 - ANNUAL STUDENT TECHNICAL SYMPOSIUM
CORPORATE SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 5th October 2026 between NextGen Brands Corp ("Sponsor") and the IEEE Student Branch Organizing Committee of Apex Institute of Technology ("Student Organizers").

1. SPONSORSHIP DELIVERABLES
1.1 The Sponsor shall provide title sponsorship of INR 1,50,000 for CampusFest 2026.
1.2 Student Organizers shall provide one 10x10 exhibition stall, sponsor logo on all main stage backdrops, and 15-minute key presentation slot.

2. PAYMENT MILESTONES AND DELAY
Payment shall be disbursed 90 days in arrears following final approval of all project deliverables and event conclusion by the sponsor marketing lead. No advance payment shall be released prior to the event.

3. ORGANIZER INDEMNITY AND PERSONAL GUARANTEE
3.1 The student club organizers personally guarantee payment and indemnify the corporate sponsor against any cancellation losses, weather delays, or low attendee turnout.
3.2 Liquidated damages shall be assessed against the student for any failure to achieve the target footfall of 3,000 registered students, deductible directly from future club sponsorship tranches.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND MEDIA RIGHTS
Sponsor retains exclusive commercial media rights to all event photographs, participant recordings, and student project pitch presentations recorded during the symposium.

5. CANCELLATION AND FORCE MAJEURE
Neither party shall be held responsible for delay or failure in performance resulting from acts of God, natural disasters, or unavoidable force majeure, except that student organizers shall refund all expended marketing costs within 14 days.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Disputes shall be resolved through good-faith mutual consultation, failing which the courts at the student's university campus city shall have jurisdiction.
